Halloween People Tier List

Ranking the types of people and houses you visit on Halloween night:

Tier Type of Person / House Description
S Tier Big House / Full-Size Candy Giver Hands out full-sized chocolate bars from a huge, decorated house. The absolute best!
A Tier Regular Candy Giver Gives out solid standard candy (fun-size chocolates, gummies, etc.). Always reliable.
B Tier Occasional Candy Giver A house or person that gives candy sometimes, but only to a few trick-or-treaters.
C Tier The Bowl Left Outside Leaves a "Take One" bowl on the porch and hopes everyone plays fair.
D Tier Bad Candy Giver Gives out candy, but it's the very bad or super unpopular kind nobody wants.
F Tier No-Candy House / Lights Off Keeps the lights off and doesn't give out any candy to the kids at all.

Best Halloween Costumes Ranked

Here is our list ranking the top costume ideas for Halloween night:

  • #1 Slasher / Movie Villains (e.g., Michael Myers): Top-tier choice! Iconic, scary, and brings authentic horror movie vibes.
  • #2 Sci-Fi / Astronaut: Detailed and fun with lots of room for creative DIY helmets and gadgets.
  • #3 Classic Vampire: A classic favorite featuring capes, fangs, and timeless Halloween style.
  • #4 Ancient Mummy: Simple yet effective spooky look using bandage wraps.
  • #5 Sheet Ghost / Mask Giver: Simple and traditional, though a bit unoriginal compared to full costumes!
